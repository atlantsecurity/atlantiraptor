---
title: Download and share what you collected
description: Download a collection or a hunt as a zip, results as CSV or JSON, and keep malware samples safe with a password.
---

Everything you collect stays on the server until you delete it. To take it
elsewhere, for a report, another tool or a colleague:

## Set a downloads password first

Open your user menu (top right), then your preferences, and set a
**downloads password**. Zips you download are then locked with it, which
stops your own antivirus from deleting malware samples, and keeps
sensitive evidence safe on the way. See
[Preferences](/console/preferences/#the-downloads-password).

## One collection

Open the collection (from the host page's **Recent collections**, or
**Collections**):

- **Download zip** on its **Overview** tab, or **All files as zip** on
  its **Files** tab, downloads everything: results, log and collected
  files.
- On the **Files** tab, download a single file.
- In the **Results** table, the export menu saves the table as CSV or
  JSON, ready for Excel or another tool.

![A collection's Files tab](/images/collection-files.webp "A collection's Files tab: each collected file with its size, and All files as zip.")

## A whole hunt

1. Open the hunt and click **Export**.
2. Choose **JSON**, **CSV** or both, and whether to password-protect the
   zip.
3. Click **Prepare export**. The zip downloads when it is ready, and stays
   on the hunt's **Exports** tab.

![Exporting a hunt](/images/hunt-export.webp "Exporting a hunt's results as JSON and CSV in a password-protected zip.")

## Keep it together: cases

To keep the collections, indicators and notes of one investigation
together, add them to a [case](/console/cases/) with **Add to case**.

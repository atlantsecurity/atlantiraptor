---
title: Get the browser history
description: Collect the websites visited and files downloaded in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox from one computer or all of them.
---

## From one computer

1. Open the computer's [host page](/tasks/look-at-a-computer/).
2. Click the **Browser history** tile. It collects the visited websites
   from Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox for every user on the computer.
3. For downloads, click **Downloads history**. Besides the browsers'
   download lists, it finds files that Windows marked as downloaded from
   the internet, with the website they came from.

Open the results under **Recent collections**. Each row is one visit:
the user, the browser, the address, the page title and the time. Type in
the filter box above the address column to look for a site.

The tiles are on Windows computers. On macOS, click **Collect** and
search for `MacOS.Applications.Chrome.History`.

## Has anyone visited this site?

You don't need to collect everyone's history to answer that. A
[Rapid hunt](/first-hunt/) for the domain checks the browser history,
recent DNS lookups and live connections of every computer in seconds, and
lists only those that match.

## From every computer

To keep the history of many computers for an investigation:

1. Open **Hunts** and click **New hunt**.
2. Select `Windows.Applications.Chrome.History` (Chrome, Edge and other
   Chromium browsers) and `Windows.Applications.Firefox.History`.
3. Choose the computers: **All clients**, or **By label** for a group.
4. Click **Create and start hunt**.

The hunt's **Results** tab shows every computer's history in one table,
which you can filter and [download](/tasks/download-results/).
